Challenge 1: Dynamic Flood Propagation & Impact 
Simulation 
Challenge: 
Extend the system to model how water accumulation can propagate between connected urban 
locations as rainfall intensity, drainage capacity, and local water levels change. Represent the 
area as a spatial/graph-based model and simulate how flooding at one location can affect 
neighboring regions over time, including the uncertainty of predicted propagation. 
Expected Outcomes: 
● Represents relationships between geographically connected locations. 
● Simulates flood/water accumulation propagation over multiple time steps. 
● Incorporates factors such as rainfall, drainage capacity, elevation or simulated terrain 
data. 
● Identifies locations likely to become affected next, not only currently affected locations. 
● Displays the predicted evolution of risk spatially and temporally. 
● Communicates uncertainty when propagation predictions have insufficient evidence.
