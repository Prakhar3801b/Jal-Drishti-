Challenge 2: Flood Response Resource Optimization 
Challenge: 
Build a response-planning layer that allocates limited resources such as pumps, rescue teams, 
barricades, or emergency personnel across multiple at-risk locations. The allocation should 
consider predicted flood severity, population/asset impact, resource capacity, travel time, 
and evolving risk, and dynamically revise the plan as new observations arrive. 
Expected Outcomes: 
● Generates a prioritized response plan across multiple locations. 
● Allocates limited resources without exceeding capacity. 
● Considers both current severity and predicted future risk. 
● Re-optimizes allocations when flood conditions or resource availability change. 
● Provides an explainable rationale for major resource-allocation decisions. 
● Demonstrates improved response coverage or reduced expected impact compared with 
a simple severity-only prioritization.
